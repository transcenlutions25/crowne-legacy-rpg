# Crowne Legacy AAA Mobile Roadmap

## Current milestone: playable vertical slice

The Blackout Contract is playable end to end on mobile web. It proves the story format, Tay's suggested-move system, open checks, persistent moral decisions, autosave, offline installation, contract ledger, and protected one-time payment loop.

## Milestone 2: canon lock and narrative production

- Import the complete Crowne Legacy character bible as the authoritative additive registry.
- Lock names, relationships, ages, visual references, speech patterns, abilities, and continuity rules.
- Write a season-level choice matrix so major decisions alter later missions, alliances, locations, available crew, costs, and endings.
- Commission or approve production portraits only from verified references.
- Expand Dawn, Kai, and the wider cast through their established canon roles. Do not substitute new characters for existing ones.

Exit requirement: every speaking role and visual asset passes a canon continuity review.

## Milestone 3: premium mobile presentation

- Select the native production runtime after a device and team evaluation.
- Build cinematic 3D or 2.5D environments, facial animation, character performance, lighting, particles, and tactile interaction.
- Add adaptive music, spatial effects, voice performance, captions, audio description support, and production haptics.
- Target stable performance profiles for low, medium, and high-tier Android devices.
- Keep the current high-contrast, large-text, reduced-motion, and no-audio-required paths from day one.

Exit requirement: a 20 to 30 minute device-tested benchmark chapter meets visual, thermal, memory, battery, and accessibility targets.

## Milestone 4: durable world simulation

- Move saves from one local slot to versioned cloud and offline-safe conflict resolution.
- Persist relationship, faction, economy, evidence, neighborhood, and legacy-decision state across the full campaign.
- Add deterministic payment and inventory transactions with server-side idempotency.
- Build content tools for quests, dialogue, world-state conditions, suggested-move rationale, and consequence previews.
- Add account recovery, privacy controls, telemetry consent, crash reporting, and live-operations safeguards.

Exit requirement: save migration, offline recovery, and transaction tests pass across supported devices.

## Milestone 5: content complete and launch ready

- Produce the launch campaign, side contracts, optional investigation routes, relationship scenes, hubs, tutorials, and multiple endings.
- Run narrative QA against the complete Crowne Legacy canon registry.
- Complete localization, ratings, legal review, privacy disclosures, store assets, closed testing, device-lab QA, and certification.
- Use a fair business model that does not sell power over moral outcomes or hide the intended story behind manipulative timers.

Exit requirement: content lock, performance lock, accessibility signoff, security signoff, and store approval.

## Non-negotiable design pillars

1. **The user's characters remain canon.** New entries are additive and never replace established characters.
2. **Tay is a strategist, not a puppet.** Every decision offers a suggested move with honest reasoning and a stated cost.
3. **Big choices change systems.** Moral decisions alter resources, relationships, control, public memory, and future content.
4. **The contract chain stays authoritative.** Acceptance, work, completion, approval, payment, and close are independently verifiable.
5. **Accessibility ships with the game.** It is not a post-launch patch.
